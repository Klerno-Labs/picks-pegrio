Steel Hardness and What It Means for Sharpening

German steel (Victorinox, Mercer) runs 56-58 HRC. It's softer than Japanese steel, which means it dulls faster but sharpens more easily with a basic pull-through sharpener. If you'll sharpen your knife monthly, German steel is forgiving. If you'll sharpen once a year, you'll be miserable either way.

Weight and Balance for Home Cooking

Professional kitchen knives are heavier to handle dense restaurant-scale volumes. For home cooking, a lighter knife (under 9 oz) reduces fatigue during 20-minute prep sessions. The Victorinox Fibrox is 8.1 oz — right in the sweet spot.

Handle Comfort Over Long Sessions

The biggest overlooked factor. A knife that hurts your hand after 10 minutes of cutting will spend most of its life in the drawer. Textured rubber handles (Victorinox, Mercer Genesis) outperform smooth plastic and pakkawood for slippery conditions.
