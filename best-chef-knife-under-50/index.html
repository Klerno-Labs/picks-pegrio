Chef knives around $50: check the exact model and current price

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

Set a delivered budget, then compare the exact knife rather than the brand name alone. Blade length, handle shape, maintenance and return conditions are more useful than a generic claim that one national style is best for beginners.

Two current references, with a budget limitation

The Victorinox Fibrox Chef’s Knife Extra Wide, 5.2063.20, lists a 7.9-inch straight-edge blade, TPE handle and 6.4-ounce weight. Victorinox identifies it as Swiss-made and not forged. Its checked U.S. price was $67 before tax on October 10, 2026, so this is not a verified under-$50 offer.

The Mercer Genesis 8-inch chef’s knife, M20608, is described by its manufacturer as forged, with high-carbon German steel and a Santoprene handle. This source identifies construction and model; it does not establish a current delivered retail price below $50.

If the budget is firm, compare genuine offers for an exact model and include tax and delivery. Do not stretch the budget solely because an older guide labeled an item a bargain. Neither linked page is a paid recommendation.

Choose fit and maintenance over a specification contest

Handle shape, blade length and balance can affect whether a knife suits your tasks. Try a comparable handle when possible, or check the seller’s return rules before opening it. We have not measured cutting performance, edge retention or comfort for either knife.

Read the manufacturer’s cleaning, storage and sharpening guidance. A steel label alone does not establish how often you will need to sharpen it. We have not verified a numerical hardness range or a universal rule that a knife below a particular weight prevents fatigue.

Compare the complete offer

Confirm model number, blade length, seller, included guard or storage and final cost. Decide whether you need a chef’s knife alone or already own another knife suitable for your intended work. A smaller purchase can be the right choice when it fills the actual gap.
