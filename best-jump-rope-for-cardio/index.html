Speed Rope vs. Weighted Rope

Speed ropes (thin cable, light handles) allow for fast rotations — 100-200 skips per minute. Weighted ropes (heavy cable, weighted handles) are slower but build shoulder endurance and burn more calories per rep. Beginners should start with speed ropes — the learning curve is steep enough without added weight.

Proper Rope Length

Stand on the middle of the rope — handles should reach your armpits. Most speed ropes come adjustable and need to be trimmed to your height before use. Rope that's too long creates timing issues; too short clips your feet.

Learning Curve for Beginners

Double unders (the rope passes twice per jump) are the goal for high-intensity cardio but take weeks to months to learn. Start with basic single-leg and alternating foot jumps until you can do 100 consecutive without stopping. Add speed before adding complexity.
