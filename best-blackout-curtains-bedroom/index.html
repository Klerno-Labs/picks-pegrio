Bedroom blackout curtains: measure the window and the whole installation

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

Measure the window, the proposed rod or track, and the drop to the intended hem before choosing curtains. The fabric is only part of the result: light can enter around the top, sides and center opening. Consider those gaps alongside color and texture.

A specific pair to compare

IKEA MAJGULL 504.178.12 is a gray blackout pair with heading tape. The listed panel size is 57 × 98 inches; the package contains two curtains. The polyester fabric is described as blackout by IKEA, but we have not measured light transmission or the darkness of an installed room.

The heading tape supports several hanging arrangements, including a rod pocket or tabs and a track with suitable gliders and hooks. Check the hardware required for the arrangement you choose; the curtain package is not a complete rail installation.

Plan coverage and care together

Allow for the folds, center overlap and side coverage you want instead of treating the flat fabric width as guaranteed coverage. Compare the mounting instructions with your wall or ceiling and use appropriate hardware. Do not obstruct ventilation, heaters or a required exit.

IKEA specifies cold washing, no tumble drying and possible shrinkage of up to 4% for this pair. Read those care directions before shortening it. A different MAJGULL length, color or article number should be checked separately.

Choose the result you need

For occasional glare control, room-darkening fabric may be enough. For a bedroom, assess the complete fitted arrangement in daylight and consider return conditions. A fabric label does not establish a sleep improvement or a fixed reduction in heating and cooling costs.
