How Picks researches and corrects guides

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Picks by Pegrio publishes shopping guides. A source check describes what a manufacturer or manual says; it is not a hands-on test. We only claim a test when there is a documented method, result and person responsible. AI assistance may be used for research and editing and is not evidence that a claim is true.

October 10 editorial correction

Earlier pages claimed tests and verified-buyer research for which we cannot substantiate records. We removed those claims across the guide library. The headphone and budget robot-vacuum pages have dated primary-source corrections. Four health-adjacent guides now remove unsupported treatment instructions and link relevant evidence instead of unverified merchant recommendations. Other pages are still undergoing specification review and say so visibly. Their earlier prices, rankings and descriptive claims should not be read as newly verified.

What a completed source review includes

Exact model names, linked primary sources, a real review date, evidence limits, useful alternatives and a correction record. Manufacturer performance claims are attributed to the maker. Unverified ratings, review counts, prices and availability are not published as structured product data.

Corrections

Send the page URL, exact model and a manufacturer or manual link through our contact page. We do not promise a response time. Original editorial revisions remain in source history so a correction does not erase the record.
