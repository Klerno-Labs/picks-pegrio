Privacy and analytics choices

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Picks offers optional Google Analytics. Until you choose “Allow analytics,” this site does not load the Google Analytics script or send our page-view, affiliate-click or outbound-click events. You can decline or change the choice using “Privacy choices” in the footer. The choice is stored locally in your browser; clearing browser storage removes it.

After consent, measurement uses property G-41TWPE6FGH. We send page origin and path, bounded public campaign labels and clicked destination domains. We omit arbitrary query strings, referrer paths, search terms, email addresses and free-form campaign labels from our custom configuration. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. Google may receive technical browser and network information when its script loads.

An outbound or affiliate click is not a purchase. Consent-based measurement undercounts people who decline or block analytics. This property is also used elsewhere in the portfolio, so reporting needs a hostname filter.

Merchant links take you to another service with its own privacy practices. Hosting providers may process requests needed to deliver the site independently of optional analytics. For questions, use our contact page.
