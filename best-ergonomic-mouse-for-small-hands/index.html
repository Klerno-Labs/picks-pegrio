Small Hand Dimensions: What You're Working With

Small hands typically measure under 17cm from wrist to middle finger tip. If your hand is in this range, mice over 120mm long will force you to grip with fingertips rather than a full palm grip, which creates more tension, not less.

Vertical vs. Horizontal Ergonomic Design

Vertical mice (like the Anker) rotate your forearm to a handshake position, reducing pronation. They work well for people with existing wrist strain. Standard ergonomic mice with sculpted shapes work better for prevention. Most small-handed users prefer standard ergonomic over vertical.

Wireless vs. Wired for Ergonomics

Wireless wins here — cable drag is a real ergonomic issue that forces compensating grip tension. If you're buying an ergonomic mouse for wrist health reasons, spend the extra $15-20 for wireless.
