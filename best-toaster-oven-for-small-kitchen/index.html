Small-kitchen toaster ovens: check usable space and clearance

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

Measure the counter space you can safely devote to an oven, then list the pans and meals it must accommodate. External dimensions, internal capacity and operating clearance are different measurements; you need all three.

One compact model to compare

The Breville Mini Smart Oven, BOV450XL, lists external dimensions of 16 × 13.6 × 8.8 inches. Its package includes a 10 × 10-inch enamel baking pan, and the manufacturer describes capacity for four slices of toast or an 11-inch pizza. These are manufacturer fit claims, not our cooking tests.

The documented eight functions are Bagel, Toast, Bake, Broil, Pizza, Roast, Cookies and Reheat. Do not infer fan-assisted convection simply from the Smart Oven name or apply features from another Breville model. Check the exact specifications if convection is essential to you.

Fit the cookware you already own

Measure the complete dish including handles and compare it with the manufacturer’s permitted interior space and cookware guidance. A slice count does not establish that a particular baking tray fits. Consider how far the door opens and where you can place hot cookware.

Read the linked manual’s placement and clearance requirements before deciding that an oven fits under a cabinet. The product’s external height is not the required operating height.

Choose functions for real meals

Decide whether you mainly want toast, reheating or small bakes. Compare the control range and included accessories for those tasks. We have not established that convection cuts every recipe’s time by a fixed percentage, or that one compact oven replaces a particular share of full-size-oven cooking.

Compare delivered price, replacement racks and trays, cleaning access and return terms. We have not measured temperature stability, browning evenness or energy consumption.
