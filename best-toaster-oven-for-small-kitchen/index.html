Convection: The Most Important Feature

Convection circulates hot air around the food, which cuts cook times by 25% and produces crispier results. For roasting vegetables, baking small batches of cookies, or reheating pizza, convection is the difference between a useful appliance and one that collects dust. All three picks above have convection; prioritize this feature above all else.

Internal Capacity vs. External Footprint

Bigger internal capacity requires a bigger external footprint. A 4-slice toaster oven is compact but limits you to half-sheet cookie trays and 9x9 baking dishes. A 6-8 slice model handles standard 9x13 pans. Measure your counter space and decide what you'll actually cook.

Element Type and Heat Distribution

Cheap toaster ovens have 2 heating elements that create hot and cold spots. The Breville Smart Oven uses 5 quartz elements with a proprietary algorithm that adjusts each element's output. The result is dramatically more even heat distribution — visible in side-by-side toast tests.
