Office desk lighting: compare monitor mounting and task coverage

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

Decide where you need light: a keyboard, paper beside the display or a larger work surface. A monitor-mounted bar can free space on the desk, while an adjustable lamp may reach an off-center task more easily. The suitable choice depends on the arrangement.

Check a monitor light’s exact fit

The BenQ ScreenBar Plus specification lists monitor thickness compatibility of 1.01–3.05 cm, USB input of 5 V/1 A and a maximum 5 W draw. It specifies a 45 cm lamp and separate desktop controller. This is a power input, not a claim that the lamp charges a phone.

The ScreenBar Plus manual is the reference for mounting and operation. Check the display’s shape and the complete mounting instructions, not only thickness. Consider webcam placement and any controls or sensors near the top edge.

Compare coverage in your setup

BenQ publishes lighting measurements under stated conditions. Those figures do not prove that your desk has the same illumination or that every screen reflection disappears. Compare the light’s position and adjustment range with the tasks you actually perform.

If you frequently work on paper beside the keyboard, a movable arm may be worth comparing. Check the base or clamp footprint, reach and power arrangement. We have not measured either design’s glare or coverage in your room.

Keep health and work claims limited

Adjustable brightness and color appearance can be useful preferences. They do not establish a particular productivity gain, headache treatment or protection from sleep disruption. We have not conducted medical or ergonomic testing.

Compare the current exact-model offer, controls, included power equipment and returns. This specification reference does not prove current stock or endorse unidentified alternative lamps.
