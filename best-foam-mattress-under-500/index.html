A foam mattress under $500: verify the size, offer and return terms

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

Define the size and complete budget before comparing mattresses. A twin starting price does not establish the cost of a queen, and delivery, taxes, a foundation or removal of an old mattress can change the total. Keep the exact model and size in your comparison.

A dated queen-size reference

On October 10, 2026, the Zinus Original Green Tea Memory Foam Mattress, queen variant 39360741441599, showed $359 before applicable taxes. The manufacturer’s current product data marked that queen variant available. This is a snapshot, not a promise of future stock or a delivered total below $500.

The current page describes a 10-inch mattress with a memory-foam layer, comfort layer and base layer. It also contains an out-of-stock notice for the older range, so confirm the selected variant’s status and delivery at checkout. Do not use these details to identify every older Green Tea mattress sold under a similar name.

Read care and return conditions first

Zinus says its mattress covers must stay on and must not be washed; removing a cover can damage the fire barrier. Follow the instructions supplied with the exact mattress. A visible zipper is not permission to remove the cover.

Read the seller’s current trial, return, foundation and warranty conditions. A trial length alone does not explain eligibility, collection, costs or what happens if the mattress is unsuitable. Keep a copy of the terms that apply to the purchase.

Compare comfort without invented scores

Consider whether you can try the mattress and how you would return it. Firmness descriptions and foam ingredients do not establish how it will feel to you. We have not measured temperature, pressure, motion transfer or long-term sagging.

No fixed foam-density threshold, guaranteed lifespan or cooling result is established here. A lower price can be worth considering when the exact product and return arrangement meet your needs.
