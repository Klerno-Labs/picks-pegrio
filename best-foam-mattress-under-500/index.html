Foam Density: The Quality Indicator

Foam density (measured in PCF — pounds per cubic foot) determines how long a mattress maintains its shape. Low-density foam (under 3 PCF) shows body impressions within 12-18 months. Quality foam is 4-5 PCF. The Tuft & Needle proprietary Adaptive Foam is 3.8 PCF — above the threshold for long-term durability. Most budget mattresses use 2-3 PCF foam that sags quickly.

Heat Retention in Memory Foam

Traditional memory foam retains body heat and creates a 'stuck' feeling. Gel-infused foam and open-cell foam designs address this with varying effectiveness. Tuft & Needle's Adaptive Foam has an open-cell structure that dissipates heat better than traditional memory foam. If you sleep hot, the T&N outperforms the Zinus Green Tea foam on temperature.

Trial Periods and Returns

Reputable foam mattress brands offer 100+ night trials with free returns. Tuft & Needle offers 100 nights. Zinus varies by retailer — Amazon orders have standard return windows, not a dedicated sleep trial. A sleep trial is essential for mattresses because you won't know how it feels for your back until you've slept on it for 3-4 weeks.
