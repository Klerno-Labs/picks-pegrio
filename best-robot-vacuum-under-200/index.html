Choose around your floor and maintenance routine

Read the manual for floor restrictions, thresholds, stairs, cords and rug handling before ordering. For pet hair, find out how the main brush comes apart and what replacement brushes and filters cost. A suction figure is not an independent carpet-cleaning score.

For an app-controlled model, check that the app is available for your phone and country and that the account and Wi-Fi requirements work for your home. For remote-operated models, consider whether physical scheduling controls meet your needs. A simpler control system can be useful if you do not want another app.

Keep the budget tied to a real offer

We have not established that all three are currently available below $200. Check the condition, seller, total price, warranty and return policy. Include replacement consumables in the comparison. A conventional vacuum may be the better purchase when stairs, upholstery or clutter are the main cleaning job.
