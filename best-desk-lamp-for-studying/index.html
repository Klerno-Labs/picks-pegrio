Study lamps: choose reach, brightness control and usable desk space

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

Look at the tasks you alternate between: reading paper, writing, drawing and working on a screen. Choose a lamp that can illuminate those areas without obstructing them. Check its reach and adjustment before choosing a particular color-temperature label.

A documented adjustable family

BenQ’s current e-Reading desk-lamp family page lists 2700–5700 K adjustment across 13 levels, a dimming knob and paper/screen modes. The page covers several lamp designs; confirm the selected model’s dimensions, mounting and package rather than assigning one set of dimensions to all of them.

Those are control options, not evidence that one setting keeps every student alert or that another prevents sleep disruption. We have not measured flicker, light distribution or visual comfort.

Check the working position

Measure where the base or clamp will sit and whether the head can reach the book or page. Try a position that limits distracting reflections and shadows in your own setup. There is no universal lamp height or left-versus-right placement that accounts for every desk and task.

Consider whether the controls are easy to reach when the lamp is positioned usefully. If you move between work areas, check the weight, cable length and storage arrangement.

Compare the complete setup

Look at the power supply, brightness adjustment, replacement-part support and actual return conditions. A manufacturer’s “flicker-free” or “eye-care” wording should not be treated as a diagnosis, clinical result or guarantee that you will not have symptoms.

If you have persistent eye discomfort or headaches, seek appropriate individual care rather than assuming a lamp is the cause or cure. This page is a buying guide, not a medical assessment.
