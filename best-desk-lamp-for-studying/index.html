Color Temperature and Your Focus

3000K (warm white) feels comfortable but can make you drowsy. 5000-6500K (cool daylight) keeps you alert but feels harsh after 2 hours. The ideal study lamp lets you switch between 4000K-5000K during active work and dial down to 3000K when reading.

Why Flicker-Free Matters

Cheap LED lamps flicker at 50-60Hz — invisible to your eyes but registered by your brain. After 2+ hours, this causes cumulative fatigue. Look for lamps marketed as 'flicker-free' with PWM-free dimming.

Arm Reach and Positioning

The lamp head should sit 15-20 inches from your work surface and positioned to the left if you're right-handed (right if left-handed) to prevent your hand casting a shadow over your work.
