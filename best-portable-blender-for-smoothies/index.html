Portable blenders: check recalls, recipe limits and cleaning first

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

A portable blender is worth considering when you need to blend away from an outlet and its permitted recipes fit your needs. Before comparing cup size or charging, check the exact model and its current recall status.

BlendJet 2 recall information

We have withdrawn the BlendJet 2 recommendation. The CPSC recall notice identifies fire and laceration hazards in affected BlendJet 2 units. Its May 13, 2025 update states that the company is out of business and the recall remedy is no longer available. CPSC instructs owners to discard the recalled product and not donate or resell it.

Read the current notice for the affected-product identification and guidance. The historical replacement instructions still pictured on that page do not override its newer no-remedy update. This guide does not direct readers to buy, repair or continue using a recalled unit.

A current model reference

The Ninja Blast BC151BK lists an 18-ounce vessel and a USB-C rechargeable motor base. Ninja describes use with fresh or frozen ingredients and ice. This is a manufacturer statement, not our test of particular recipes or a claim that it outperforms other portable blenders.

Check its manual for ingredient preparation, liquid requirements, fill limits, charging and cleaning. USB-C describes the connector; it does not establish a full-charge time or prove compatibility with every charger. Confirm what cable and power equipment are included or required.

Plan the whole portable workflow

Consider where ingredients will be stored, where you can rinse and clean the vessel and how the blade assembly must be secured during travel. A rinse is not a substitute for the manufacturer’s cleaning process. Do not use hot or carbonated ingredients unless the exact model expressly permits them.

Compare available replacement vessels, the complete packed size, battery-care instructions and current delivered price. If you mainly mix a powder into liquid, a shaker bottle may meet the need without a powered blade; if you need larger or demanding recipes, compare a suitable countertop appliance.
