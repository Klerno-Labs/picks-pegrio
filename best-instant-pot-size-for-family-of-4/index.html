6-Quart for Family of 4: When It's Enough

A 6-quart Instant Pot comfortably handles 4 servings of soup, stew, chili, or pasta. It fits a 3-4 lb chicken or roast. If your meals are standard dinner portions and you don't batch cook, the 6-quart is the right size. It's also lighter (11 lbs vs. 18 lbs for the 8-quart) and easier to store.

When to Buy the 8-Quart

Buy the 8-quart if you cook for 5+ people, batch cook large quantities for the week, or frequently make stocks and broths. A 6-quart maxes out at 4-5 lbs of meat. If you're breaking down whole chickens or cooking 6-bone short ribs, the 6-quart is too small.

Model Differences: Duo vs. Duo Plus vs. Pro

The base Duo has 7 functions and is the best value. The Duo Plus adds a steam release valve and more precise controls. The Pro adds a sterilize function and steam cooking mode that the others lack. For a family of 4 cooking standard recipes, the base Duo 6-quart is the right call — spend the savings on a quality cookbook.
