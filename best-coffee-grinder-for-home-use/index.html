Burr vs. Blade Grinders: Why It Matters

Blade grinders chop randomly, producing a mix of powder and large chunks that extract at wildly different rates. The result is simultaneously over-extracted bitter compounds and under-extracted sour compounds — a muddy, unpleasant cup. Burr grinders crush beans between two abrasive surfaces at a consistent gap, producing uniform particle sizes.

Grind Settings for Different Brew Methods

French press needs coarse (Baratza setting 28-30). Pour over needs medium-coarse (18-22). Drip coffee needs medium (15-18). Espresso needs fine (1-5). A grinder with fewer settings (blade grinder, basic burr) limits which brew methods you can dial in properly.

Dosing Consistency

Timed dosing (grind for X seconds) varies based on bean density. Weight dosing (add beans until scale reads correct dose) is more accurate. The OXO Brew uses timed dosing with a reliable timer — consistent enough for most home use. The Baratza Encore is timed but extremely consistent due to motor stability.
