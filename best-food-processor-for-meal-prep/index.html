Meal-prep food processors: match the attachment to the job

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

List the repetitive tasks you want to shorten: chopping, slicing, shredding or mixing. Then identify the blades, discs and permitted batch sizes each task requires. A large bowl and a high wattage number do not prove that every preparation fits.

A documented full-size model

The Cuisinart Custom 14, DFP-14BCNY, lists a 14-cup work bowl, 720 W motor, 4 mm slicing disc, medium shredding disc and chopping/mixing blade. Its published dimensions are 11 × 7.9 × 14.8 inches. Check the exact suffix; this review does not establish the package of another Custom 14 variant.

Those specifications identify the supplied tools. They do not prove how many meal portions fit or establish a minimum wattage that every processor needs for a particular vegetable.

Check working capacity and feed preparation

Read the linked manual and recipe guidance for maximum quantities, liquids, dough and each attachment. Nominal bowl capacity is not a license to fill it completely for every operation. Check the permitted ingredient size and use the required pushers rather than improvising around the feed-tube controls.

Compare the included disc thicknesses with your preferred cuts. If you need another result, confirm a compatible accessory exists and include its cost. A mini chopper may be enough when you only want small chopped batches and do not need slicing discs.

Plan cleaning and storage

Identify the parts you will wash after a normal recipe and where sharp components can be stored as instructed. Cuisinart says this model’s parts other than the motor base are dishwasher-safe; follow the exact manual’s placement and handling guidance.

Compare replacement bowls, lids and discs as well as delivered price and warranty terms. We have not timed cleanup, measured output uniformity or established that one bowl shape is easier to wash.
