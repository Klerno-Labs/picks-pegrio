Temperature Accuracy: How Close Is Close Enough

The Anova and Joule both hold temperature within ±0.1°F — effectively perfect for cooking purposes. The Inkbird holds within ±0.5°F. For most sous vide cooking (steaks, chicken, fish), ±0.5°F is imperceptible. The premium precision of Anova/Joule only matters for egg cooking and extremely delicate fish.

App vs. Manual Control

The Joule Turbo requires the app for temperature setting — there's no manual control panel. This is a deal-breaker if your phone battery is unreliable or you dislike app-dependent appliances. The Anova has both app and manual control. The Inkbird has both.

Container Requirements

You need a vessel that holds water at the cooking temperature for hours without warping. A 6-quart Dutch oven or cambro container works. Most people sous vide in a large pot — a 8-12 quart stockpot is ideal. You'll also need a lid or plastic wrap to reduce evaporation on long cooks.
