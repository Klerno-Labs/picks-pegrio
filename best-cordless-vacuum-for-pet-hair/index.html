Cordless vacuums for pet hair: match the heads, floor and maintenance

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

Start with where the hair collects: hard floors, rugs, upholstery or stairs. Compare the tools included in the exact package and whether you can comfortably use and maintain them. A high advertised suction number alone does not establish pet-hair pickup in your home.

Check the V15 model and package

The current Dyson V15 family page distinguishes V15 Detect and V15 Detect Absolute packages. It describes a Digital Motorbar head with anti-tangle combs and a Fluffy Optic head for hard floors. The Absolute listing also identifies HEPA filtration and a built-in dusting and crevice tool; do not transfer every package feature to every V15.

The same U.S. page states a two-year limited warranty, with linked terms. It does not support the former five-year claim. Its maximum runtime is a conditional manufacturer estimate, not a promise for every powered head, floor or mode.

Plan routine maintenance

Check how the brush, bin and filter are maintained in the matching manual. Look at replacement-filter and battery availability, access to tangled hair, and where the charger will fit. A tool marketed to resist tangling still needs inspection and care.

If upholstery is the main problem, confirm that a suitable small powered head is actually included. Consider the weight in handheld use and the reach needed for stairs. We have not compared pickup, noise, handling or runtime across brands.

Separate filtration from health outcomes

A filtration specification is not proof that a vacuum prevents or treats allergies. Choose according to documented equipment features and individual needs, without assuming a numerical particle claim establishes a clinical result.

At the October 10 check, both V15 packages on the linked family page were marked out of stock. That describes those direct listings, not every retailer. Check an exact current offer and authorized-seller warranty before buying.
