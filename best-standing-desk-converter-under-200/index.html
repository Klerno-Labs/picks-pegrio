Desk converters: measure seated and standing fit before buying

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

A desktop converter must work when lowered as well as raised. Measure your existing desk, the equipment it must carry and the keyboard position you need before comparing the lifting mechanism or advertised price.

Match dimensions to the selected variant

The FlexiSpot AlcoveRiser M7 family page presents 28-, 32-, 35- and 40-inch choices. The visible default specification is for the 28-inch version: a 4.7–20.1-inch height range, 28.3 × 16.3-inch top and 28.3 × 12.2-inch keyboard tray. It lists a suitable desk size of at least 29.5 inches wide and 25.6 inches deep. Do not apply those values to another variant.

The separate 35-inch M7M listing is marked Off Shelf at this check. It remains a product reference, not a confirmed current purchase offer. A family page’s default or dynamically displayed price is also not a quote for the size you selected.

Check the complete movement

Compare the base footprint with usable desk space, including fixed stands and cable openings. Check whether the converter moves straight upward or toward you. Leave the clearance and cable slack required by its manual, and confirm whether the keyboard tray interferes with your seated position.

List the weight of the monitor, stand or arm, laptop and accessories. Check separate main-surface and tray limits and whether your monitor arm is permitted. A combined marketing load figure is not a substitute for those instructions.

Compare the delivered setup

Confirm the exact model, assembly steps, current delivered total and return freight. If you cannot establish a usable seated and standing position, a different desk arrangement may be a better option than adding height to the existing surface.

We have not measured stability, lifting force or ergonomic outcomes. A converter is not established here as a treatment for pain or a guarantee that long periods standing are suitable for you.
