Scissor Lift vs. Post-and-Arm Design

Scissor lift converters (Flexispot M2B) are more stable at all heights because the weight distributes across two legs. Post-and-arm designs are less expensive but wobble noticeably with dual monitors. If you'll use this with a single laptop, post-and-arm is fine. Dual monitor setups need scissor lift.

Height Range That Covers Your Needs

You need at least 16 inches of height adjustment to go from sitting ergonomic to standing ergonomic for most desk setups. Check that the minimum height is low enough for your sitting position — this is frequently an issue for taller desks (30+ inch height) where the converter starts too high.

Weight Capacity for Your Setup

A 27-inch monitor weighs 10-15 lbs. A laptop adds 3-5 lbs. External speakers, docking station — it adds up. Most converters rate for 30-35 lbs. The Flexispot M2B is rated for 33 lbs, which handles two 24-inch monitors comfortably.
