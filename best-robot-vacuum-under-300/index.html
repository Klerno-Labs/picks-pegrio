Robot vacuums under $300: check the exact bundle and navigation

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

Set the budget for the complete package: robot, required charging equipment and any optional emptying dock. Compare the exact model name and included items. A low price for a robot alone does not establish the cost of a self-emptying bundle.

What the original Q5 documentation establishes

The Roborock Q5 series page describes LiDAR navigation, maps, room routines, no-go zones and virtual walls. It distinguishes the original Q5 and Q5+ presentation and shows Auto-Empty Dock Pure support. Check the actual bundle contents rather than interpreting dock support as a dock being included.

The page’s 2700 Pa figure is a manufacturer suction specification. It is not our cleaning result and does not establish how the robot compares across rugs, debris types or competing suction measurements.

Check your floors and obstacles

Measure clearances beneath furniture and review thresholds, rugs, cables and areas that need to be excluded. A mapping feature does not prove that a robot will detect every small object or hazard. Follow the matching manual’s preparation and operating guidance.

Consider whether you need room selection, scheduling or operation without a phone. Check the current app, network and account requirements for those features before purchasing. Read the product’s privacy information if household mapping is a concern.

Verify an offer before choosing

This review did not verify a current original Q5+ bundle below $300. The former $279 purchase recommendation is withdrawn. A specification page remaining online does not establish retail stock, and Q5 Pro or another later model needs its own review.

Compare the delivered price, seller warranty, replacement brushes, filters and any dock bags. We have not measured navigation efficiency, pickup, noise, battery life or the time between dock-bag changes.
