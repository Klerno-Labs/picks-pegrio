Why Gooseneck Spout Matters

A standard kettle pours too fast and with too wide a stream for pour over control. Gooseneck kettles let you pour in slow, controlled circles — which is how you evenly saturate the coffee bed and avoid channeling. This directly affects extraction quality.

Temperature Accuracy

The Fellow Stagg EKG holds temperature within ±1°F and maintains it for 60 minutes. Budget kettles with temperature display often drift ±10°F from their setting. For light roasts that need precise 205°F, that gap is audible in the cup.

Capacity for Multiple Cups

Most single-cup pour over uses 300-400ml of water. A 600ml kettle handles two cups in one fill. The 1L capacity of the Bonavita handles a full Chemex. Match capacity to your typical brew size.
