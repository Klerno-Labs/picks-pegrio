Pour-over kettles: compare the spout, controls and useful capacity

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

Choose a kettle around the volume you brew and the pouring control you want. A narrow spout may suit a slow pour-over recipe, while a wider spout may be more convenient for other kitchen tasks. Neither design guarantees better coffee.

Identify the current version

The current Fellow Stagg EKG listing offers Pro and Pro Studio Edition versions. It lists a 0.9-litre capacity, 104–212°F temperature range and selectable hold periods of 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. These are manufacturer features, not our measurements of temperature accuracy.

The checked dimensions are 11.10 × 6.77 × 7.7 inches, with a U.S./Canada Type-B plug. Confirm the selected version, power rating and region before ordering. Do not assume accessories or bases for an older EKG are interchangeable with a Pro.

Separate connectivity from remote operation

Fellow’s updater explanation says the app is for firmware updates, not remotely switching the kettle on, changing temperature or controlling settings. App connectivity is therefore not a reason to expect phone-operated brewing.

Choose capacity and controls for your recipe

Allow for the water you use to brew and rinse according to your method, while observing the kettle’s minimum and maximum fill marks. Compare whether you need a hold function, scheduling or a simple boil. A displayed setting does not establish the exact temperature at the coffee bed.

Use the kettle only as its instructions permit. Fellow identifies these electric kettles as water-only appliances used with their electric base, not on a stovetop. Compare handling, cleaning access, delivered price and the actual warranty terms.
