What the Research Actually Shows

A 2021 Cochrane Review found insufficient evidence that blue light glasses reduce eye strain symptoms compared to regular glasses. What does reduce eye strain: taking 20-second breaks every 20 minutes (20-20-20 rule), increasing font size, reducing screen glare, and ensuring proper monitor distance (arm's length from your face).

For Sleep Quality: They Actually Work

Blue light's effect on sleep is better established. Blue light in the 415-455nm range suppresses melatonin production. Wearing blue light glasses 2 hours before bed — even cheap ones — is supported by research as a meaningful sleep quality intervention.

Lens Tint: Clear vs. Yellow vs. Orange

Clear lenses with AR coating block 10-20% of blue light. Yellow-tinted lenses block 50-70%. Orange lenses block 90%+. For daytime computer use, clear lenses are fine. For evening use, yellow or orange provide the meaningful melatonin protection.
