Standing desks under $300: price the complete desk and check its range

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

Compare complete desks, not just frames. Record the desktop size and material, frame, controller, delivery and any required accessories. A starting price below $300 does not establish that your chosen configuration stays within that budget.

Keep E7 and E7 Pro specifications separate

The FlexiSpot E7 page includes a comparison with E7 Pro. Its E7 section lists dual motors and a 22.8–48.4-inch frame height range; the comparison labels that range as excluding the desktop. The top and any casters change the finished working height.

The page also distinguishes a T-shaped E7 frame from the C-shaped E7 Pro. Do not assign the Pro’s capacity, cable arrangement or dimensions to a standard E7 order. Check the exact frame version and matching manual, since the page contains material for more than one model.

Check the working range before the load number

Measure the height you need for seated and standing work with your keyboard and screen arrangement. Check knee space, desktop depth and monitor-arm mounting. The lowest frame setting is not the same measurement as the top surface after assembly.

Consider the total equipment and desktop load using the model’s instructions. A static load rating is not a tested guarantee of stability at every height, and a motor count alone does not rank wobble or noise.

Verify the budget and use conditions

This review did not verify a complete E7 configuration delivered for $299. The former fixed-price recommendation is withdrawn. Compare the current cart, tax and delivery costs before deciding whether the model belongs in your under-$300 shortlist.

Read assembly and operating instructions, leave the required clearance and route cables for the full movement. We have not tested lifting, stability, noise or long-term durability, and this guide does not prescribe a universal sit/stand timetable.
