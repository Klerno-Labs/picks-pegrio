Adjustable dumbbells: recall checks and model selection

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

This guide corrects unsupported product and safety claims. Sources are linked where used. It is not a hands-on test or a personal suitability assessment. This revision used AI-assisted research; there are no affiliate purchase links on this page.

The previous guide recommended BowFlex SelectTech dumbbells without addressing an existing recall. That recommendation has been withdrawn. Product safety and exact model identity come before adjustment speed or maximum weight.

BowFlex 552 and 1090 recall

The CPSC recall dated June 5, 2025 concerns weight plates that can detach during use. The agency directs owners of recalled units to stop using them and obtain the applicable remedy. BowFlex’s current U.S. recall information explains product identification and the claim process. This page no longer recommends the recalled SelectTech models. Check the exact model and generation; a similar number on a newer replacement does not establish its identity.

Compare the equipment you would actually receive

Ask for the complete model number, generation, instruction manual, included trays and whether the price covers one dumbbell or a pair. Check the smallest and largest weight, each available increment and the assembled dimensions at the weights you use. A large maximum weight can be less useful than appropriate increments for your exercises.

Compare how the selection mechanism is checked and maintained, what the manual says about handling, the warranty exclusions and availability of replacement parts. We have not tested adjustment speed, locking reliability or drop resistance. The former claim that one mechanism is inherently more durable was not supported by a comparative test.

Choose around your space and routine

Measure storage and exercise space, including access to the trays. If possible, try the grip and movement with suitable equipment before committing. Fixed-weight dumbbells are an alternative when you need only a small selection of weights; we have not ranked a specific replacement product.
