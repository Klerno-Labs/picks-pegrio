Whey vs. Plant-Based Protein

Whey protein (from milk) is the most bioavailable complete protein — it contains all essential amino acids in ratios that support muscle protein synthesis. Plant-based proteins (pea, rice, hemp blends) are complete when blended but have slightly lower bioavailability. For lactose intolerant users, plant-based is the practical choice. For everyone else, whey is the more efficient option per gram of protein.

Protein Per Serving: What to Look For

Most quality powders deliver 20-25g of protein per serving. Anything under 15g per serving with a 30g scoop contains excessive filler (carbs, fat, or non-protein amino acids). Check the protein percentage: divide protein grams by serving size in grams. Above 60% is acceptable; above 75% is excellent.

Digestive Comfort

Bloating and gas after protein shakes are common with whey concentrate, which contains residual lactose. Whey isolate (ON Gold Standard) has the lactose removed and is tolerated by most lactose-intolerant users. Plant proteins can cause bloating from fiber and legume compounds — try individual varieties to find which agrees with your digestion.
