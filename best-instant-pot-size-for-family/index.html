3-Quart: For 1-2 People

The 3-quart Instant Pot handles 2-3 servings of soup, steamed vegetables, and rice. It's compact enough for small kitchens and light enough to move easily. It cannot fit a whole chicken or roast. If you're cooking for one or two people and don't batch cook, the 3-quart is the right size and costs $30 less than the 6-quart.

6-Quart: The Default Choice

The 6-quart handles 90% of family cooking scenarios for up to 6 people at weeknight portion sizes. It fits a 3-4 lb whole chicken, 4-5 lbs of ribs, or a 6-quart batch of soup. This is the size to buy unless you have specific reasons for larger or smaller.

8-Quart: For Batch Cooking and Large Families

If you meal prep for 5+ days, regularly cook for 6+ people, or make large bone broths, the 8-quart is worth the extra $40. It's also 7 lbs heavier when full and takes longer to come to pressure, which adds 5-10 minutes to recipes compared to the 6-quart.
