Eye Cup Design for REM Sleep

Contoured or cup-style sleep masks (Manta) create a cavity over your eyes that doesn't contact your eyelids. This is important for REM sleep because your eyes move rapidly during this phase — pressure from a flat mask can interrupt this movement and reduce sleep quality. The Manta's adjustable, independent eye cups are the best design for frequent flyers.

Material: Silk vs. Foam vs. Cotton

Natural silk (Alaska Bear) is breathable, hypoallergenic, and cool on skin. Foam contoured masks (many budget options) trap heat. Cotton is comfortable but less light-blocking due to weave gaps. For travel where you'll wear the mask for extended periods, silk outperforms foam on comfort. For pure light blocking, foam contoured masks are more effective.

Strap Comfort for Side Sleepers

Standard elastic straps create a pressure point at the back of the head for side sleepers. The Manta Sleep Mask uses a wide adjustable strap. For sleeping on a plane in an upright or slightly reclined seat, any mask will work. For lying sideways on a flat bed, the strap design matters significantly.
