Coffee makers under $100: verify the offer before the feature list

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

Start with the final delivered total, then decide which features are essential. A coffee maker advertised below $100 can exceed the budget after tax, shipping or required accessories. An old sale price is not a current offer.

Keep the budget attached to a real listing

The checked OXO Brew 9-Cup Matte Black, 8725000, has a manufacturer reference describing a thermal carafe and programmable wake-up timer. We have not confirmed a currently available delivered offer below $100 for this model.

This reference helps explain the feature tradeoff, but it is not a recommendation to exceed your budget. We have not established a current delivered under-$100 offer for the other models formerly listed here, so there are no purchase rankings or affiliate offers in this revision.

Choose the features you will use

Decide whether you want one mug or a batch, an automatic start time, and a removable water tank. Check what the manufacturer means by a “cup”; the advertised count may not match your mug. If you need a small batch, look for instructions that explicitly support it.

Compare a glass carafe and warming plate with an insulated carafe according to how long you keep coffee and how you clean the vessel. We have not measured holding temperature, flavor or extraction quality.

Check ongoing costs and space

Identify the required paper or reusable filter, cleaning supplies and replacement carafe. Measure the machine plus the clearance needed to fill the tank and lift the basket under cabinets. A compact footprint may still require space above it.

Before checkout, record the complete model code, seller, delivered price, included accessories and return rules. Confirm any certification with its issuing program for that model rather than inferring it from another brewer in the range.
