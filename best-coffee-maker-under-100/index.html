Why Brew Temperature Is the Critical Spec

The OXO Brew and Bonavita both earned SCA (Specialty Coffee Association) certification, which requires consistent 197.6-204.8°F brew temperature. The Cuisinart DCC-3200 reaches 200°F on its Brew Strength setting. Sub-$50 drip makers typically fail this standard, which is why coffee from cheap machines tastes flat even with expensive beans.

Carafe Type: Thermal vs. Glass

Thermal carafes keep coffee hot for 2+ hours without a heating plate. Glass carafes with heating plates cook the coffee and turn it bitter after 30 minutes. If you don't drink your entire pot within 20 minutes, buy a machine with a thermal carafe. Both OXO and Bonavita use thermal.

Bloom Function for Better Flavor

Pre-infusion (bloom) wets the grounds before full brewing begins, releasing CO2 that otherwise creates uneven extraction. The OXO Brew has an automatic bloom function that pauses brewing for 30-45 seconds after initial water contact. This noticeably improves cup quality, especially with fresh-roasted beans.
