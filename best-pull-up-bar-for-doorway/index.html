Lever Design vs. Doorframe Mounting

Lever-style pull-up bars (Iron Gym style) hook over the door frame and use your body weight as counterforce — no installation required. Screw-mounted bars require permanent installation but are more stable for heavy users (200+ lbs). For most home gym setups, lever-style is the right call.

Weight Capacity Reality Check

Most doorway bars are rated for 300 lbs. In practice, the rating assumes static dead hang, not dynamic kipping or aggressive pull-up momentum. If you do kipping pull-ups or weigh over 220 lbs, buy a screw-mount bar or a free-standing pull-up station instead.

Door Frame Compatibility

Most lever-style bars work with standard 24-28 inch doorway widths. Unusually wide or narrow doorways require checking the bar's range before purchase. The Perfect Fitness Multi-Gym fits 24-32 inch doorways and is the most compatible with non-standard framing.
