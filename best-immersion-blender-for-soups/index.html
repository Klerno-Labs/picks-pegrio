Immersion blenders for soup: check the pot, temperature and controls

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

Compare an immersion blender with the pots you use and the recipes you intend to make. Shaft reach, grip, permitted temperature and cleaning instructions matter as much as the motor specification. Do not assume every hand blender is suitable for boiling soup.

A documented model and its attachments

The Breville Control Grip, BSB510XL, lists an 8-inch immersion depth, stainless steel shaft, 42-ounce jug, 25-ounce chopping bowl and whisk attachment. Breville describes its bell-shaped base as reducing suction. That is a manufacturer claim, not our comparison of splashing or control.

The BSB510 manual gives the operating sequence, speed controls and separate cleaning rules. Match the document to the appliance and follow its hot-liquid, container and run-time precautions.

Check reach without guessing from pot volume

Measure the depth and shape of the vessel and compare them with the appliance’s permitted immersion level and handling instructions. A six-quart pot is not one universal depth. Ensure the working position can be maintained without putting the motor body into liquid or improvising around the controls.

If hot soup is the main use, read that procedure before buying. Do not infer that more watts permit longer continuous use or eliminate cooling requirements. We have not established a wattage threshold for smooth soup or tested splash resistance.

Pay for attachments you will use

A chopper or whisk can be useful, but each adds parts to store and clean. The manual distinguishes dishwasher-compatible components from the motor body and attachment gearboxes. Follow those individual instructions rather than treating the complete appliance as dishwasher-safe.

Compare the current package, replacement parts, delivered cost and returns. A comfortable-looking trigger is not proof that it fits your grip; try a comparable design if possible.
