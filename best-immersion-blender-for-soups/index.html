Wattage for Different Soup Textures

600W handles most pureed soups including butternut squash, potato leek, and tomato bisque. 800W+ handles thicker blends and allows longer continuous blending without overheating. Under 300W (most cheap models) struggles with fibrous vegetables and requires repeated short bursts.

Shaft Length for Tall Pots

A standard immersion blender shaft is 8 inches. This reaches the bottom of most 6-quart stockpots. Deeper pots (8-12 quart) need a 10-inch shaft. Check your most commonly used pot depth before buying.

Splash Guard Design

The Breville Control Grip has a patented bell-shaped blade guard that creates suction around the blending area, dramatically reducing splash. This is the most important functional difference between the Breville at $89 and cheaper alternatives. For anyone who has cleaned pureed soup off their ceiling, it's worth the premium.
