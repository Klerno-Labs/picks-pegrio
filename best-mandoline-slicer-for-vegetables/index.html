Mandoline slicers: match the cuts and follow the exact guard instructions

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

We checked the current manufacturer sources linked below. We have not tested these products. Features are manufacturer statements; buying considerations are editorial judgment. This revision used AI-assisted research. All external links on this page are direct, unpaid references.

Decide which cuts you need before choosing a mandoline. Straight slices for a small meal, repeated thickness changes and additional blade patterns are different jobs. Compare setup, storage and cleaning as well as the blade arrangement.

A compact slicer and a larger adjustable model

The OXO Good Grips Hand-Held Mandoline, 1119100, offers three thickness settings: 1, 2.5 and 4 mm. Its listed dimensions are 11.5 × 3.75 × 0.5 inches. Consider that compact format if its straight-slice function meets your needs; this page does not establish julienne capability.

The OXO V-Blade Mandoline, 1155700, has interchangeable blade arrangements, adjustable thickness, folding feet and a food holder. Check the current package and manual for the cuts you intend to make. More components also mean more pieces to identify, clean and store; we have not timed either workflow.

The food holder is part of the method

The 1155700 manufacturer manual directs users to always use the food holder and warns that its blades are very sharp. Follow the setup, handling, cleaning and storage instructions for the exact model. Keep it away from children.

A cut-resistant glove is not a replacement for the prescribed holder or a guarantee against injury. We have not tested gloves, guards or the relative injury risk of these products. If a food cannot be controlled as the manual requires, choose another preparation method instead of improvising a grip near the blade.

Check whether the extra features are useful

List the vegetables and cuts you prepare repeatedly. If a knife or another tool already meets that need, another slicer may not be worthwhile. If you need repeated slices, check the model’s thickness choices, permitted foods and storage arrangement before paying for extra blades.

For the V-Blade model, the linked manual separates blade hand-washing from the cleaning instructions for its other parts. Do not apply a generic dishwasher claim to every component or to another model. Compare delivered price, spare-part availability and return conditions; we have not verified a fixed budget or long-term sharpness.
