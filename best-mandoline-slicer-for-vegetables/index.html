Safety First: Cut-Resistant Gloves Are Non-Negotiable

Buy a pair of cut-resistant gloves the same day you buy a mandoline. The food holder grips small vegetables poorly, and the reflex to catch a falling piece toward the blade is fast enough to cause serious injury. Gloves at $15-20 prevent ER visits. This is not optional.

Blade Quality: Japanese Steel vs. Stainless

Japanese mandolines (Benriner) use extremely thin, sharp blades that create cleaner cuts with less force. European-style blades (OXO) are thicker and more durable but require more pressure. Thin blades stay sharper longer; thicker blades handle tougher vegetables (beets, winter squash) better.

Adjustability: Fixed vs. Variable Thickness

The Benriner has a screw adjustment for thickness — precise but fiddly to calibrate. OXO has a dial with preset thicknesses — less precise but fast and reliable. For most home cooks, OXO's dial system is more practical even though Benriner's adjustment is theoretically more accurate.
