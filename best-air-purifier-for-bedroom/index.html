Bedroom air purifiers: compare room sizing and filter costs

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

Start with the room you want to clean and the setting you expect to use overnight. The largest advertised coverage figure is not useful unless you know how many air changes per hour it represents. Then compare the replacement filters, controls and return conditions.

Two identifiable models to compare

Coway’s Airmega Mighty AP-1512HH lists automatic speed adjustment, eco mode, a washable pre-filter, deodorization filter, HEPA filter and bipolar device. Its published coverage is 361 square feet at 4.8 air changes per hour, versus 1,748 square feet at one air change per hour. Those are different sizing conditions for the same machine.

Levoit’s Core 300-P, identified as model Core 300 in its specifications, lists three-stage filtration, a 143 CFM CADR and 222 square feet at 4.8 air changes per hour. Its stated dimensions are 8.7 × 8.7 × 14.2 inches. This is not a verification of the similarly named Wi-Fi Core 300S-P.

Consider the Coway when automatic fan adjustment matters to you. The Levoit provides a compact option with simpler controls. This is an editorial distinction based on features, not a finding that one cleans better in your bedroom.

Check the setting you will actually use

Coway publishes a 24–53 dB(A) range; Levoit publishes 24–54.5 dB. We have not measured either unit or established a comparable test setup. The lowest noise figure does not promise maximum airflow at that setting. Ask which fan speed supports the room-size claim you plan to use, and consider indicator lights, placement and return conditions.

Price the filters before ordering

Coway lists six months for its deodorization filter and one year for HEPA filter life expectancy. Check the actual manual and condition-based instructions; those intervals are not a guarantee for every environment. Identify the correct replacement filter and current price for either candidate, and confirm which parts can be cleaned rather than replaced.

Compare complete model names, included filters, delivered price and warranty seller. This guide does not establish an allergy, asthma or sleep-treatment benefit.
