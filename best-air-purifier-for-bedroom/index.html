CADR Ratings Explained Simply

CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate) measures how much clean air the unit delivers per minute. The rule of thumb: multiply your room's square footage by 1.5 to get the minimum CADR you need. A 150 sq ft bedroom needs a CADR of at least 225. The Levoit Core 300 delivers 230.

Sleep Mode Noise Levels

Look for noise ratings below 25 dB for sleep mode. This is quieter than a whisper and won't disturb light sleepers. The Coway Mighty in sleep mode runs at 24.4 dB — audible as white noise but not disruptive. Avoid any model above 30 dB for sleep mode.

Filter Replacement Costs

Annual filter costs are the hidden expense. HEPA filters for the Levoit Core 300 run about $20/year. The Winix uses a washable pre-filter (helps the HEPA last longer) plus a True HEPA at ~$30/year. Factor this into the total cost of ownership.
