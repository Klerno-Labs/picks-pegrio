Fitness trackers under $100: price checks and buying tradeoffs

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

A $100 limit is useful only if it covers what you plan to spend. Separate the device price from sales tax, shipping, replacement bands and any app subscription. The two manufacturer listings below illustrate different buying situations; a low displayed price is not evidence that a product is available.

Inspire 3: consider the total cost and phone requirement

Google documents connected GPS, so route tracking depends on a paired phone. Its specification page lists an AMOLED display and up to 10 days of battery life, with results depending on settings and use. The current requirements name the Google Health app, Android 11 or iOS 16.4 or later, and Bluetooth plus internet access for syncing.

This is an option to investigate if those requirements suit you and the final price fits. Check which desired features are included without Premium and the current renewal terms before accepting a trial. Our evidence does not establish that this band counts steps or sleep stages better than its competitors.

Band 7: a lower listed price, but no direct stock

Amazfit specifies a 1.47-inch AMOLED screen, the Zepp app and phone-connected GPS. Its battery estimates are up to 18 days under typical use or 12 under heavy use. Those are manufacturer scenarios, not our measurements. The sold-out status means we cannot present the $49.99 listing as an immediately purchasable recommendation.

If you find one elsewhere, verify that it is the same model, check the seller’s warranty and return policy, and ask whether it is new, used or refurbished. Do not transfer one seller’s price or warranty to another offer.

What to check on any budget tracker

  • GPS: decide whether carrying your phone is acceptable. A connected-GPS specification does not promise independent route recording.
  • App costs: make a list of the features you want and confirm which need payment. Compare the first-year total, not just a trial offer.
  • Fit and controls: compare the listed wrist sizes and return conditions. Screen dimensions cannot prove that text will be comfortable for you to read.
  • Battery settings: look at the conditions behind the advertised maximum. Plan charging around when you want to wear the band.

Do not shop by an unsupported accuracy score

We withdrew the earlier numerical heart-rate error ranges and step-count percentages because this guide had no supporting tests. Sleep scores also should not be used to diagnose the cause of a bad night. The AASM’s 2018 position statement explains why consumer sleep data needs clinical context; it does not rank these products.
