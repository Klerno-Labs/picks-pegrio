ANC Performance: The Real Gap

The honest difference between $80 and $300 ANC headphones is low-frequency cancellation. Cheap ANC kills mid-range office noise well. It struggles with airplane engine hum, HVAC rumble, and bass-heavy environments. If you're primarily in an office, sub-$150 is genuinely fine.

Battery Life Claims vs Reality

Manufacturers test battery at 50% volume with ANC off. Real-world with ANC on at 70% volume typically runs 20-30% shorter than the spec. The Anker Q45's claimed 50-hour battery is real at low volumes; expect 35-38 hours with ANC fully engaged.

Microphone Quality for Calls

This is where budget ANC headphones still lose. The $79 options sound fine for listening but make you sound like you're calling from a cardboard box. If you take calls, budget for the $150+ tier.
