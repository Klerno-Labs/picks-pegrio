For calls, read the package details

The Evolve2 55 is an on-ear work headset, while the other models are over-ear listening headphones. That design difference can matter more than a battery headline. Jabra lists USB-C charging and a carry pouch; a desk stand is SKU dependent. Our earlier statement that a charging case was included was incorrect. Its page currently mentions different Link adapter numbers in the FAQ and specifications, so confirm the adapter supplied with the exact SKU.

We cannot rank microphone quality, long-session comfort or office-noise reduction from these specification sheets. A model’s support for two Bluetooth connections also does not guarantee every device-switching behavior you might expect. Check the instructions for your phone, computer and conferencing setup.

Before ordering an older model

Check whether the offer is new, renewed or used; who supplies the warranty; whether pads and batteries can be serviced; and what returns allow after a fit test. A low advertised price is less useful if the exact model is unavailable or its condition is unclear. You can also compare current models at a local retailer where fit and returns are easier to establish.
