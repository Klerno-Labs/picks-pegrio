Budget adjustable dumbbells: check recalls and the complete price

Sources checked October 10, 2026 · By Picks by Pegrio

Evidence note — October 10, 2026

This guide corrects unsupported product and safety claims. Sources are linked where used. It is not a hands-on test or a personal suitability assessment. This revision used AI-assisted research; there are no affiliate purchase links on this page.

A low advertised price is not enough to establish a useful dumbbell set. Check recall status, whether the price covers one weight or a pair, and the total cost of a setup that meets your needs.

BowFlex 552 and 1090 recall

The CPSC recall dated June 5, 2025 concerns weight plates that can detach during use. The agency directs owners of recalled units to stop using them and obtain the applicable remedy. BowFlex’s current U.S. recall information explains product identification and the claim process. This page no longer recommends the recalled SelectTech models. Check the exact model and generation; a similar number on a newer replacement does not establish its identity.

Make the budget comparison consistent

For each candidate, write down whether the offer is one dumbbell or two, the included weight range, required trays or stands, shipping, tax and return freight. Compare the total for the equipment you need. Do not treat an occasional or unverified discount as a standing under-$200 offer.

Then compare the weight increments, overall dimensions, grip, locking checks and maintenance instructions for the exact model. Ask about replacement parts and warranty exclusions. A brand name alone is insufficient: similar listings can describe different weight ranges and adjustment systems.

Alternatives within a fixed budget

A smaller set of fixed weights or a documented plate-loaded system may cover your intended exercises. Compare the complete setup and storage requirement rather than assuming every adjustable design replaces the same rack of weights. We have not verified a currently available set that meets every reader’s budget or training needs.
