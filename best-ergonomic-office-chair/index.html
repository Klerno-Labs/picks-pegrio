Why Herman Miller and Steelcase Command Premium Prices

The Aeron and Leap V2 use patented materials and mechanisms refined over decades of ergonomic research. The Aeron's PostureFit SL supports both sacrum and lumbar curve independently — a feature no sub-$500 chair replicates correctly. Both chairs carry 12-year warranties and can be rebuilt if components fail. Over 10 years, the per-year cost is $130-150.

The Mid-Range Sweet Spot: $400-600

The Branch Ergonomic Chair, Humanscale Freedom, and Autonomous ErgoChair Pro deliver 80% of flagship ergonomics at 30-40% of the cost. They lack the material refinement and fine adjustment range of Herman Miller, but for users who don't have existing back issues, they're genuinely excellent.

What to Adjust First

Most people set their chair height and never touch it again. The order of adjustment: (1) seat height so feet are flat on the floor, (2) lumbar support position to the natural curve of your lower back, (3) armrest height to support elbows at 90 degrees, (4) backrest recline tension. Most chairs default to too-stiff tension.
